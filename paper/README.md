# Paper

The manuscript, "Boundary B-Spline Mimetic Schemes: Degree-Sized Wall Algebra, Spectral-Like Resolution, and Compatible Flow" by Yong Yi Bay and Kathleen A. Yearick, is in preparation. The source and PDF will be added here when it is submitted.

Every figure and table in the manuscript is produced by a driver in `code/` (`s2_*` to `s6_*`, `make_*_figure.py`) from the result files in `code/results/`.
